# 03 — Homepage: Hero + animated stats

**What to build:** A visitor landing on the homepage is immediately met with a premium full-screen hero and, on scrolling, sees the company's track record presented as animated counters.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Project scaffold & site shell

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Full-screen hero section with a subtle animated background, large bold heading (e.g. "Building Quality. Delivering Trust." or equivalent), and supporting paragraph
- [ ] Primary CTA "Get a Free Quote" and secondary CTA "View Our Work", both using elegant motion/micro-interactions
- [ ] Animated stat counters for Years Experience (20), Projects Completed (250+), and Happy Customers (200+) — no "Areas Covered" stat
- [ ] Counters animate into view on scroll (not on initial load) and only fire once
- [ ] All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`
- [ ] Section is fully responsive across mobile/tablet/laptop/desktop
